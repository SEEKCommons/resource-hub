# SPARQL Templates

- `author_topic_works(topic_qid)` – Fellows with works on topic **T**; returns (person QID, work QID, title, year, DOI).
- `person_outputs(person_qid, type)` – Publications/datasets/software for **P**.
- `initiative_network(initiative_qid, depth)` – Projects/people/venues linked to **I**.
- `venue_recent(venue_qid, year_from)` – Recent items at **V** since year.
